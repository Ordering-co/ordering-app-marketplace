# Ordering Marketplace App

Open your customer app on your Ordering project and see your first edit live.

## 1. Paste this into your coding agent

```text
Get https://github.com/Ordering-co/ordering-app-marketplace.git and read setup.md. Follow its instructions to configure my Ordering project, open the app locally, and verify my first visible edit through Fast Refresh.
```

## 2. Confirm your project

Confirm your Ordering project with the agent, or provide its project code.

## 3. Open your app

Check that the preview shows your project. Ask the agent to make one small visible edit and watch it update live.

Local iOS preview needs macOS and Xcode; Android development needs Android Studio and Java.
